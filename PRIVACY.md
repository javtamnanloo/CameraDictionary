# Privacy Policy: Camera Dictionary

*Last updated: 7 November 2026*

Camera Dictionary is a free Android app made by Javad Tamnanloo. This policy explains what
happens to your information when you use it.

**In short:** your photos and your saved words stay on your phone. Only the single word you
choose to look up leaves it.

## Camera and photos

The app uses the camera only while the Scan screen is open. When you press **Read**, the
current picture is analyzed **on your phone** by Google's ML Kit text recognition. Pictures are
never saved to your storage and never uploaded. A picture is discarded when you start a new
scan or close the app.

## Words you look up

To show a definition, the app sends the one word you selected or typed, over an encrypted
(HTTPS) connection, to:

- the **Free Dictionary API** (api.dictionaryapi.dev), and, if that service doesn't answer,
- **Wiktionary** (en.wiktionary.org), run by the Wikimedia Foundation.

Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address with each request. The app
sends no name, account, or device identifier. Their own privacy policies apply:
[Free Dictionary API](https://dictionaryapi.dev/) and
[Wikimedia Foundation](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy).

## Saved words (your Leitner box)

Words you star are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, together with their
definitions, the line of text where you found them, and their review schedule. If Android
backup is turned on, Android may include this list in your device backup in your Google
account, so it can be restored on a new phone.

You can delete one word, or all of them, at any time in the app. Uninstalling the app deletes
them as well.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows at most one reminder a day, and only when words are
due. This is worked out on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

## Google ML Kit diagnostics

ML Kit may send Google basic diagnostic information, such as the device model, Android version,
and performance data, so Google can maintain and improve the service. It does not send your
pictures or the text in them. Details:
[ML Kit data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).

## What the app does not do

The app has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking, and it does not sell or
share your data.

## Children

Camera Dictionary is a general-audience reference tool. It does not knowingly collect personal
information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

## Contacting the developer

The app's **Email me**, **Send a suggestion** and **Report a problem** buttons open your own
email app with a new message. Nothing is sent unless you press Send there. A problem report
starts with the app version, Android version and phone model, to help find the cause; you can
change or delete any of it before sending. If you write, I receive your email address and your
message, and use them only to reply and to improve the app.
